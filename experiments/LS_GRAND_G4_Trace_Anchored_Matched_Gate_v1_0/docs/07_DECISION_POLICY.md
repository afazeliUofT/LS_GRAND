# Decision policy

## READY_FOR_FOCUSED_SUBSTANTIAL_PAPER_PROGRAM

Authorizes a narrowly scoped paper program and independent reproduction.  It
does not authorize field-defining language, deployment claims, or patent
freedom-to-operate statements.

## CONTINUE_WITH_EXTERNAL_ALARM_ONLY

Conditional recovery survives but the internal code-residual trigger does not.
Proceed only with a real CPE/tracker alarm.

## NARROW_TO_ONE_STRUCTURED_CODE_PAPER

Only one code/application survives.  Drop the general code-agnostic narrative.

## STOP_APPROXIMATE_ROUTE

A strengthened matched receiver erases the advantage.  Stop algorithmic,
hardware, and broad-theory investment.

## INCONCLUSIVE_TRACE_EVENT_COUNT

The carrier-recovery trace did not yield enough events.  Do not force slips to
manufacture a positive result.

A positive gate may be earned through tail-work superiority at compatible reliability or through a material reliability gain with bounded (at most twofold) wall-time slowdown.
