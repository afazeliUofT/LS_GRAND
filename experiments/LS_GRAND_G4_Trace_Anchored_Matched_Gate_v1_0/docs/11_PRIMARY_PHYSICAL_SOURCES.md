# Primary physical-source anchors

## Pilot-symbol phase unwrapping

H. Cheng, Y. Li, M. Yu, J. Zang, J. Wu, and J. Lin,
“Experimental Demonstration of Pilot-Symbols-Aided Cycle Slip Mitigation for
QPSK Modulation Format,” *Optical Fiber Communication Conference*, paper
Th4D.1, 2014, DOI: 10.1364/OFC.2014.Th4D.1.

The Optica abstract reports 28-Gbaud single-polarization QPSK, 2-MHz combined
linewidth, fourth-power carrier-phase estimation, and 1.56% pilot overhead.
These values anchor the primary software trace and optimistic pilot-rate bound.

## Measured event-rate relevance

C. R. S. Fludger, D. Nuss, and T. Kupfer, “Cycle-slips in 100G DP-QPSK
Transmission Systems,” *Optical Fiber Communication Conference*, paper OTu2G.1,
2012, DOI: 10.1364/OFC.2012.OTu2G.1.

The Optica abstract reports real-time Viterbi--Viterbi cycle-slip measurements
with Poisson mean probabilities spanning 1e-12 to 1e-3 under ASE and nonlinear
phase noise.  This establishes physical relevance while also showing that the
event rate is highly platform dependent.

## Claim limitation

These sources support parameter selection and the existence of the impairment.
They do not validate the generated trace as a measured dataset, do not establish
that LS-GRAND is beneficial, and do not support deployment claims.
