# Executive novelty decision

## Decision

**PASS_NARROWED_APPROXIMATE_CLAIM_FREEZE**

The numerical evidence at commit `ed5a4b4be5dbabeed98691043dd64db6d0415dae` justifies one more bounded
research gate, but only after abandoning the broad foundational exact-decoding
novelty thesis.

## What is no longer a defensible novelty pillar

1. Marginalizing codeword likelihoods over hidden states is established by
   BCJR and sum-product inference.
2. The original sum-of-current-queue-heads stopping rule is a communication
   specialization of the Threshold Algorithm for top-1 sum aggregation.
3. Code-aided phase-rotation selection and cycle-slip handling are established
   in both the literature and patent record.
4. Candidate-list construction followed by complete noncoherent or state-marginal
   rescoring is established.
5. The current exact certificate failed its practical-usefulness gate.

## Narrow candidate that remains worth testing

The remaining candidate is the **specific combination** of:

- a zero-or-one persistent intra-packet QPSK automorphism jump;
- global likelihood ordering of state-conditioned word components;
- an arbitrary code-membership oracle;
- first-valid operation with an adaptively invoked two-codeword marginal list;
- algebraic orbit-safety screening; and
- event-triggered operation with tail-complexity accounting.

No identical disclosure was found in the frozen scientific search, but every
individual ingredient has close prior art.  The claim must therefore remain
narrow, combination-based, and provisional.

## Authorization

- One structured-code/matched-baseline/physical-model gate: **authorized after
  review of this freeze**.
- Broad theory, custom code family, continuous phase-noise generalization,
  hardware, multi-user extensions, or field-defining publication campaign:
  **not authorized**.
- Patent freedom to operate: **not determined**.
