# Post-gate actions

## If `CONTINUE_TO_FINAL_PHYSICAL_MATCHED_GATE`

Freeze one real anchor—preferably coherent optical QPSK residual cycle slips or
a defensibly parameterized pilot-limited wireless burst—and implement:

1. measured, hardware-emulated, or standard-derived slip/event statistics;
2. event-triggered C4 forward-backward/BCJR plus a strong structured decoder;
3. stronger code-specific state-sweep decoding (for example Chase-list BCH and
   CRC-aided polar list decoding);
4. equal-net-rate pilot-assisted and differential alternatives;
5. at least 200 relevant errors or a preregistered confidence-width stop;
6. p50/p90/p99 work and no-slip overhead on one common optimized platform.

A final substantial-paper decision is made only after that gate.

## If `CONTINUE_CONDITIONAL_ON_EXTERNAL_ALARM_ONLY`

Do not tune the internal alarm. Obtain a defensible tracker alarm/flag and repeat
only the event-triggered comparison.

## If `NARROW_TO_APPLICATION_ONLY`

Do not pursue a general method. Determine whether one code/use case supports a
focused application note.

## If any stop verdict occurs

Archive the exact oracle and rank-compression evidence, then stop the algorithmic
route. Do not begin hardware or broad theory.
