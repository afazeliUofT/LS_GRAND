# Novelty boundary and unresolved formal gate

A limited primary-source update on August 5, 2026 confirms several close
neighbors:

- Miyamoto and Yang, arXiv:2501.12971, finite-state **additive** universal noise
  guessing;
- Li and Zhang, arXiv:2603.08325, exact/approximate GRAND for Gaussian ISI;
- Razeghi, arXiv:2607.28363, exact likelihood ordering for correlated Gaussian
  noise with low pathwidth;
- Geiselhart et al., arXiv:2305.01972, phase-equivariant polar coded modulation;
- Cui et al., arXiv:2604.07004, burst-aware iterative LDPC decoding for bursty
  phase noise.

These sources make broad claims such as “first GRAND with memory,” “first phase-
aware coding,” or “first exact correlated-channel GRAND” indefensible.  The
narrow candidate claim remains the finite latent-transformation **fiber** over
codewords, exact aggregation or controlled approximation of that fiber, and an
unseen-codeword certificate.

This package does not certify novelty.  A formal claim chart must still search
GRAND and non-GRAND terminology, optical cycle-slip receivers, hidden-state
sequence decoding, stack/list algorithms, code-aided synchronization, and
patents.  No numerical pass can substitute for that gate.
