## Narrow claim that remains authorized

For a frozen zero-or-one persistent QPSK phase-jump model, we study a code-agnostic latent-cause guessing receiver that globally orders state-conditioned candidate components and invokes an arbitrary code-membership oracle. The practical modes are first-valid decoding and an adaptively invoked two-codeword list with complete state-marginal rescoring. We measure actual noisy rank compression, tail work, and affine orbit safety against matched state-aware receivers.
